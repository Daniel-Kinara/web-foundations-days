About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking page for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-do items before you forget them. It is made for students and busy people who want a fast, distraction-free way to keep their thoughts organised.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and find the "Add a note" form.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" box.
  3. Click the "Add Note" button to save it.
  4. Check the "Your notes" list to see everything you have captured.
  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Live note count
  • Clean, distraction-free layout
